# abrahao2026-carbon

ESM2025 T13.2

Scenarios from the preprint Abrahao et al. (2026) "Carbon cycle uncertainties and the role of land-use change emissions in ambitious mitigation pathways". All scenarios are designed to remain under 1.7K warming under assumptions on the carbon cycle informed by different DVGMs. The assumptions can inform just the land carbon densities (DenEff) or both the densities and land sink response, affecting the budget (DenBudEff).
